About

I am an M.S. student in the School of Computing at KAIST, advised by Hongseok Yang. I also received my B.S. there in 2025, with a double major in Mathematical Sciences. I am interested in formalized mathematics and extremal combinatorics, and currently work on machine-checked flag algebra proofs in the Lean theorem prover.

  • Formalized mathematics
  • Interactive theorem proving
  • Flag algebras
  • Extremal graph theory
